## Radius

Four radii, on the 4px grid. The bigger the element, the rounder its corners, so nested corners stay parallel: a button
(`rounded-m`) inside a card (`rounded-l`) inside a dialog (`rounded-xl`).

## Elevation

Shadows are for what floats above the page, and nothing else. Cards and panels sit on the page with a border.
